   Earlier this year, my colleagues and I set out to solve a puzzle: Why

   money?
   [33]Christopher Flavelle

   Christopher Flavelle

   Trump’s fascination with icebreakers can be traced to a chance
   encounter in Helsinki more than 30 years ago, when the future president
   was on a business trip.
   [34]Christopher Flavelle

   Christopher Flavelle

   Fast forward to March 2025, when Trump won a golf tournament alongside
   Alexander Stubb, the president of Finland. Afterward, Trump announced
   an agreement to buy icebreakers.

   Christopher Flavelle

   That deal eventually grew to be 11 icebreakers, twice as many as the
   Coast Guard previously said it needed. And those vessels come at a high
   cost. The contracts are worth almost $7 billion.

   the norms around government spending, in pursuit of projects that
